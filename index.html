Work project · team-based · Healthtech

Integrated Pharmacy Management and Medication Safety Transformation

Problem
High medication error rates, a severe inventory blind spot, financial leakage, inefficient clinical operations, compliance and risk exposure, and a lack of analytics.
My contribution
Led a team of Healthtech Business Analysts in project discovery, analysed assigned business challenges, identified operational gaps and contributed to solution recommendations. Consolidated findings with the team into the final report and presentation.
Approach
A structured, patient-centred, evidence-based analysis covering medication safety risks, inventory visibility, financial leakage, workflow inefficiency, regulatory compliance and reporting limits, and evaluating system integration, automation and data-driven decision-making.
Outcome
A business analysis recommending an integrated pharmacy management and medication administration system, with critical gaps identified in medication safety, inventory, billing and regulatory compliance.

Work project · team-based · Healthtech

Prior-Authorization Workflow Optimisation and Healthcare Digital Transformation

Problem
Excessive turnaround time, fragmented submission channels, manual data entry, disconnected systems, an inefficient clinical review workflow, no real-time status visibility, delayed patient access to care, claims inefficiencies and heavy administrative workload.
My contribution
Took part in discovery, analysed assigned challenges, identified bottlenecks and contributed to root cause analysis, process improvement and requirements documentation. Worked with the team on digital solution recommendations, the final report and the presentation.
Outcome
Delivered a Gap Register, Root Cause Analysis, As-Is and To-Be process maps and a Business Requirements Document (BRD). Recommended an integrated solution targeting a 92.3% reduction in turnaround, from 26 hours to 2 hours.
